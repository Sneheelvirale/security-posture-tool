5. Methodology, Limitations & Ethics

Methodology

This assessment utilizes a qualitative maturity evaluation framework based on the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) v1.1. Controls are evaluated on a 6-tier maturity scale (0 = Not Implemented to 5 = Optimising). Evidence verification ensures that scores reflect verifiable organizational practices rather than self-reported assertions.

Limitations & Educational Scope

Important: This assessment was conducted without active penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, or external network probing. The findings represent point-in-time compliance against baseline controls for a fictional small organisation profile. This tool is designed for educational evaluation under university supervision.
